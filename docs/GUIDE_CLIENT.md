# SPEC User Guide - CLIENT Role
## Specimen Transmittal Form (STF) Management

---

## Overview

As a **CLIENT** user in SPEC, your role is to:
1. Create Specimen Transmittal Forms (STF) for sample submission
2. Add patients to the STF
3. Add procedures for each patient (e.g., CBC, Urinalysis)
4. Book the transmittal to send samples for testing

Your workflow: **Create STF → Add Patient → Add Procedure → Book Now**

---

## 🔑 Login to SPEC

1. Open the SPEC application in your browser
2. Enter your **email or username**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **"Sign In"** button
5. You'll be taken to the main dashboard

---

## 📋 Step 1: Create a New STF (Specimen Transmittal Form)

### Navigate to STF Section
1. From the main dashboard, look at the **left sidebar**
2. Click on **"STF"** or **"Transmittal"** section
3. You'll see the **STF List** page showing all existing STFs

### Create New STF
1. Click the **"+ Create STF"** or **"New STF"** button (top-right corner)
5. **Success message** appears
6. You're now in the **STF Details** page, ready to add patients

---

## 👥 Step 2: Add Patient to STF

### View STF Details Page
After creating STF, you'll see:
- **STF ID** - Your transmittal identifier
- **STF Date** - Date created
- **Facility** - Your facility name
- **Patient List** - Currently empty
- **Action Buttons** - "+ Add Patient" button

### Add New Patient
1. Click the **"+ Add Patient"** button
2. A modal or form will appear with patient fields:

**Patient Information Fields:**
- **Patient First Name** * - Patient's first name
- **Patient Last Name** * - Patient's last name
- **Patient ID/Medical Record Number** (Optional) - If available
- **Date of Birth** (Optional) - Patient's DOB
- **Age** (Optional) - Patient's age
- **Gender** (Optional) - Male / Female / Other
- **Contact Number** (Optional) - Patient's phone

3. Fill in at least the **First Name** and **Last Name** (required fields marked with *)
4. Add other information if available
5. Click **"Add Patient"** or **"Save"** button
6. Patient appears in the **Patient List** on the STF

### Patient List Display
After adding patients, you'll see a table showing:
- **Patient Name** - Name of patient
- **Patient ID** - Medical record number (if added)
- **Actions** - Buttons to manage patient (View Details, Delete)

---

## � Step 3: View Patient Details

### Access Patient Details
1. From the STF Details page, find the patient in the **Patient List**
2. Click **"View Details"** button or link for that patient
3. **Patient Details** panel opens showing:
   - All patient information entered
   - **Procedures List** - Currently empty
   - **Add Procedure** button

### Review Patient Information
1. Verify all patient information is correct
2. If information needs to be updated:
   - Click **"Edit"** button (if available)
   - Modify the information
   - Click **"Save"** to update

### Go Back to STF
1. Click **"Back to STF"** button
2. Returns to the main STF page with patient list

---

## 🗑️ Step 4: Delete Patient from STF (if needed)

### Remove a Patient
1. From the STF Details page, find the patient in the **Patient List**
2. Click **"Delete"** button or trash icon for that patient
3. A **confirmation dialog** appears asking:
   - "Are you sure you want to delete this patient from the STF?"

4. Click **"Yes"** or **"Confirm"** to delete
5. Click **"Cancel"** if you changed your mind
6. Patient is removed from the STF

**Note:** You can delete patients at any time before booking the STF.

---

## 💊 Step 5: Add Procedures to Patient

### Navigate to Patient Details
1. From the STF Details page, click **"View Details"** for the patient
2. **Patient Details** page opens
3. You'll see:
   - Patient information
   - **Procedures List** - Currently empty
   - **"+ Add Procedure"** button

### Add New Procedure
1. Click the **"+ Add Procedure"** button
2. A modal or dropdown will appear showing available procedures:
   - **CBC** (Complete Blood Count)
   - **Urinalysis**
   - **Blood Chemistry**
   - **Glucose Test**
   - **Lipid Panel**
   - **Thyroid Function Test**
   - **COVID-19 Test**
   - **Other procedures** (depending on your facility)

3. Select the procedure you want to add
4. If available, you may need to specify:
   - **Test Type** (if multiple options)
   - **Specimen Type** (Blood, Urine, etc.)
   - **Quantity** (if applicable)
   - **Special Instructions** (Optional)

5. Click **"Add"** or **"Confirm"** button
6. Procedure appears in the **Procedures List**

### View Added Procedures
The procedures list will show:
- **Procedure Name** - Name of the test (e.g., CBC)
- **Specimen Type** - What specimen is needed
- **Status** - Added/Pending
- **Actions** - Options to modify or remove

### Add Multiple Procedures
1. Repeat the "Add New Procedure" steps for each additional test
2. You can add as many procedures as needed for the patient
3. All procedures will be included when you book the STF

### Remove a Procedure
1. If you need to remove a procedure, click **"Remove"** or delete icon
2. **Confirmation dialog** appears
3. Click **"Yes"** to confirm removal
4. Procedure is removed from the list

---

## ✅ Step 6: Back to STF and Book Now

### Return to STF Details
1. From the Patient Details page, click **"Back to STF"** button
2. Returns to the main **STF Details** page
3. You'll see the updated patient list with procedures

### Review Before Booking
Before clicking "Book Now", verify:
1. ✓ All patients added to the STF
2. ✓ Each patient has procedures selected
3. ✓ All patient information is correct
4. ✓ No unnecessary patients in the list
5. ✓ All required procedures are added

### Book the STF
1. Click the **"Book Now"** button (usually at the bottom or top-right)
2. A **confirmation dialog** appears:
   - "Are you ready to book this STF?"
   - Shows summary of patients and procedures
   - Confirms facility and date

3. Review the summary
4. Click **"Yes"** or **"Confirm Booking"** to finalize
5. **Success message** appears:
   - "STF successfully booked!"
   - Shows booking confirmation number
   - STF Date, Facility, Number of Patients, Number of Procedures

### After Booking
1. STF status changes to **"Booked"** or **"Submitted"**
2. You can view **Booking Details** showing:
   - Booking Date/Time
   - Confirmation Number
   - Patient Count
   - Procedure Count
   - Facility information

3. You're ready to submit samples to the lab based on this STF

---


## ⏱️ Typical Process Duration

| Step | Duration | Your Action |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Create STF** | 1-2 min | Fill form & submit |
| **Add Patient** | 2-3 min per patient | Enter patient info |
| **Add Procedure** | 1-2 min per procedure | Select procedures |
| **Review & Book** | 2-3 min | Verify & click "Book Now" |
| **Total Time** | **8-15 minutes** | For 2-3 patients with 3-4 procedures |

## 💡 Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Enter accurate patient information
- Double-check patient names and details
- Verify procedures before booking
- Review STF completely before clicking "Book Now"
- Keep track of your STF booking numbers
- Contact lab team if you have questions about procedures

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mistake patient identities
- Add unnecessary procedures
- Leave required fields blank
- Book without verifying information
- Use placeholder/fake patient data
- Add patients you don't plan to submit samples for
